This policy describes how Warriors of the Fifth Sun (W5S) handles information during its Quest playtest, including account linking, friends, parties, multiplayer, voice and optional playtest feedback. W5S is intended for teens and adults aged 13 and older.

Information used by the game

  • Player identity. Unity Authentication creates a W5S player identifier and stores authentication credentials needed to recognize that player. We use your chosen player name. When native Meta social is enabled in a Quest release, the Meta integration checks app access and obtains your app-scoped Meta identifier, profile information such as your gamertag, and a platform user proof. Your Meta identifier and proof are passed to Unity Authentication to link your existing W5S account. W5S does not ask for your Meta account password. Native Meta social availability depends on the installed playtest build; the Meta social processing described here applies when that integration is enabled.
  • Friends and parties. When native Meta social is enabled, W5S uses eligible friend identifiers, names and online status returned by Meta, and your Meta blocked-user list. W5S also maintains its separate Unity friends, requests, blocks and parties. Party information includes membership, leader, invitation and destination information. Meta invitations carry expiring information needed to enter the intended private W5S party.
  • Multiplayer activity. Connected players and the game host exchange player names, team membership, game actions, scores and avatar movement, including headset and controller poses. Network providers and hosts process connection information, such as network addresses and session identifiers, to connect players and operate matches.
  • Voice. When microphone permission and the game's transmission controls allow it, your voice is sent through Unity Vivox to players in the relevant team or proximity channel. Vivox processes a player identifier, display name, connection/device metadata and information used for positional voice. The current game integration uses live audio channels and does not implement its own voice recording or transcription feature.
  • Local settings and playtest records. Your device stores control, language, comfort and audio preferences, recent-player information and authentication state. Optional local conduct reports contain the reported name, connection identifiers, reason and match context; these reports do not contain recorded voice. Local comfort/performance exports include measurements such as frame performance and calibration settings.
  • Optional submitted statistics and support material. Pressing the session statistics send button submits performance summaries, device model, quality mode, game protocol and a generated report identifier to the current match host. The host adds a receipt timestamp and connection identifier and saves the report. These submissions do not include a Meta identifier or gamertag in their report payload. If you separately share screenshots, recordings, logs or messages for support, we receive the information contained in them.
  • Former private ALPHA update emails. We previously used approved testers' release-channel email addresses and saved language preferences to send build announcements and room-code corrections through Gmail. We discontinued these tester emails and deleted the associated messages and drafts from the developer's Gmail account on October 30, 2026. Developer-held historical notification records remain subject to the retention and request-handling process below. Stopping these emails does not remove your Meta release-channel access.

Why information is used

Information is used to recognize players, preserve their W5S identity when linking Meta, show eligible friends, deliver and accept party invitations, uphold blocks, connect multiplayer sessions, transmit voice, save preferences, investigate problems and evaluate playtest performance. Historical notification records document past private ALPHA announcements and related preferences. The current playtest configuration does not enable Unity Ads or Unity Analytics.

Who receives information

Meta provides platform identity, entitlement, social presence and invitation services. Unity provides Authentication, Friends, Lobby, Relay and Vivox services used by W5S. They receive the information needed for those services. Your Meta identity and user proof are shared with Unity Authentication for account linking; W5S does not send your Meta friends list to Unity Friends as a list of W5S player IDs.

Other players receive information needed for play, such as your visible name, avatar movements, team and transmitted voice. Hosts receive gameplay/network information and statistics you choose to submit. When you join a player-hosted, LAN or separately operated dedicated server, that operator may also handle session information. Avoid putting private information in your public player name or voice communications.

Google previously processed recipient addresses and message content for the former private ALPHA emails. We no longer use Gmail for tester announcements, and the associated messages and drafts were deleted from the developer's Gmail account on October 30, 2026. Google's own retention and deletion practices are described in Google's privacy policy.

Meta and Unity explain their platform/service practices in the Meta Platforms Technologies privacy policy and Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. Those policies supplement this game's policy. Service providers may process information in countries where their services operate.

Storage and retention

Preferences, cached identity and local playtest files remain on the device until cleared or otherwise removed. Clearing app data or uninstalling can remove local files and may affect access to an unlinked W5S identity; it does not delete server-side service records.

Unity Authentication identity and account links remain until deleted through the applicable account-management process. Friends and block records persist through the relevant service until changed or deleted. Party and match state is maintained while needed for those sessions; service logs can have separate retention periods.

We keep private ALPHA tester email addresses, language preferences and delivery history while the person is an active tester, then delete those records within 90 days of the person leaving the test. We retain a minimal email opt-out record for as long as needed to avoid sending announcements to someone who asked us to stop. This retention process is handled manually.

We delete developer-held playtest statistics, reports, logs and support recordings within 90 days of collection unless needed for an active investigation or legal obligation. We review retained investigation files when the investigation closes and remove them when no longer needed. This process is handled manually; the current playtest does not automatically expire these files. Live voice is processed through Vivox; see Vivox's privacy and retention overview for its service metadata and live-audio handling.

Your controls and requests

You can use the game's voice controls, microphone permission, mute and block controls, and choose whether to send session statistics or share support material. Meta's account privacy settings also affect the social information the app can retrieve. Access to online services is needed for features that depend on them.

For requests concerning access, correction or deletion of W5S data, contact hectormeneses333@hotmail.com. Include your W5S player identifier if available and the information needed to locate the relevant record. Do not send account passwords, authentication tokens or app secrets. Account ownership may need verification before records can be disclosed or removed.

The developer personally handles requests, verifies account ownership as needed, and checks the relevant Unity services, developer-held files and notification records to locate and address the requested W5S data. Deleting only the Authentication account does not establish that every service's records were removed. Requests about your underlying Meta account can also be made through Meta's own account controls.
